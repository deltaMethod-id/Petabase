Documentation and Methodology

Petabase publishes database popularity rankings using public source data. This page explains where the numbers come from, how they are interpreted, and how often you should refresh them.

Primary Data Source

The ranking table in stats.html is based on DB-Engines popularity scores (July 2026 snapshot) and database model labels as reflected in public ranking mirrors and DB-Engines category documentation.

Reference links: db-engines.com/en/ranking, HelloGitHub DB ranking mirror.

What The Score Represents

DB-Engines popularity scores are composite indicators. They are not direct active-user counts. The score combines multiple external signals to represent overall market visibility and momentum.

  • General web search interest
  • Technical discussion activity
  • Job-market and professional demand indicators
  • Social and community presence signals

Update Cadence

Rankings are updated monthly by DB-Engines. Petabase should be refreshed monthly to keep the ordering and scores accurate. For procurement, architecture standards, or migration planning, always validate against the latest month.

Scope Notice

Petabase tracks popularity and ecosystem traction. It does not replace benchmarking, security review, pricing analysis, or workload-specific testing.
